# Product vision

Ghahvehto connects Iranian coffee consumers, independent cafés and future educators, suppliers and brands through discovery, experiences, community and rewards. Persian RTL first; internationalization is architectural, not a later rewrite. Domain ghahvehto.com and Instagram @ghahvehto are founder controlled; hosting and accounts are not assumed.

Core consumer and café services remain free forever. Future revenue comes only from genuinely new optional advertising, analytics, integrations, education and commerce. No guaranteed free coffee, ranking, or unfunded star redemption.

Initial success means verified active cafés and repeat genuine interactions, not imported directory volume. No public marketing until iOS, Android and the public website are actually available to intended users. Store availability in Iran is a launch-critical unresolved gate.
